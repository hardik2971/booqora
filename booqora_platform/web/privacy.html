Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Applies to the Booqora web platform and the Booqora mobile apps

1. Who we are

Booqora is rental-management software that lets rental businesses ("Merchants") manage bookings, inventory, customers, documents, payments and an online store. This policy explains what data Booqora processes, why, and the choices you have. Contact: support@booqora.app.

3. How we use data

  • To provide, maintain and secure the Booqora service.
  • To send transactional emails (booking confirmations, payment receipts, documents) on a Merchant's behalf.
  • To process payments through the payment provider a Merchant has enabled.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Service providers

Booqora runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Hosting), with data stored in Google Cloud's asia-south1 (Mumbai) region. Payments are processed by Razorpay, Stripe and/or PayPal under their own privacy policies. Transactional email is delivered through an SMTP email provider. These providers process data only to deliver the service.

5. Merchants and their customers

For customer and booking data that a Merchant manages in Booqora, the Merchant is responsible for that data and Booqora processes it on the Merchant's behalf. If you are a Merchant's customer, contact that business directly to access or delete your information.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Access is restricted by security rules so that each workspace's data is only available to its own team members. Payment secrets are held server-side in Google Secret Manager.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account or workspace is active. You can delete records in the app at any time. To delete your account and workspace data, email support@booqora.app from your account email; we complete deletion within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records (for example invoices).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Children

Booqora is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email. The effective date above shows the latest version.
